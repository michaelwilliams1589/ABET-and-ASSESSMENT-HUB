2. Discussion

The meeting considered approving a new Project Management assessment mapping for ETDE 3213 — Project Management & Engineering Economics so that outcome evidence for Fall 2026 and later uses the Final Project Presentation rather than the Email Assignment.

Rationale recorded for this summary (synthesized at Michael Williams’s request; not claimed as prior factual meeting history or as an ABET mandate): the previous Email Assignment was narrow SO 3 evidence. The new team project, scored with a common rubric, supports systematically observing technical problem-solving (SO 1), oral technical communication (SO 3), and individual team contribution and leadership (SO 5) in an applied context. Shared group points remain separate from individual outcome ratings. A faculty review gate ensures records are not treated as official prematurely. The intended result is stronger, more consistent individual evidence for continuous improvement.

Prior performance records remain intact. Existing Program Educational Objectives and Student Outcomes remain as previously reaffirmed; the next regular PEO/SO review is scheduled for 2028. Work Breakdown Structure, Gantt schedule, Bill of Materials, and economics content continue as course performance evidence and are not assigned a new program outcome or invented official performance indicator. Shared group points alone do not establish individual attainment. The Hub individual threshold remains 70% on the direct 0–5 scale (a rating of 4 or 5 meets it; 3 or below does not). The program target remains 75% of assessed students meeting the threshold.

This minutes document does not assert that ABET or an advisory board approved the mapping change, and it does not assert that attendees reviewed the confidential self-study source PDF.

3. Motion and vote

Michael Williams moved to approve replacing the Email Assignment → SO 3 mapping with the Final Project Presentation → SO 1 / SO 3 / SO 5 mapping (version etde3213-final-presentation-v1, selected November 6, 2026), effective Fall 2026, with the Email Assignment retained as inactive historical mapping so original records keep their meaning.

Result: Motion carried unanimously. All listed attendees voted yes. No seconder was recorded.

1. Approved outcome statements and evidence methods

The Final Project Presentation rubric determines the course grade: the team’s group score out of 80 plus each student’s individual score out of 20. The direct evidence ratings below are separate from that course grade. Each assessed student must be considered individually in each outcome actually assessed.

Approved outcomeCourse performance evidence and direct assessment method
SO 1 — Technical problem-solving
An ability to apply knowledge, techniques, skills and modern tools of mathematics, science, engineering, and technology to solve broadly-defined engineering problems appropriate to the discipline;		Direct individual method: Ask each student to explain a technical choice, test result, or corrective action. Enter a direct individual Technical rating on the 0–5 evidence scale and record a short observation.

Supporting rubric evidence: Technical Design & Execution (/12), Challenges & Problem-Solving (/8), Final Results & Demonstration (/10), and individual Project Knowledge (/5). These team/category scores support—but do not replace—the individual rating.
SO 3 — Communication
An ability to apply written, oral, and graphical communication in broadly-defined technical and non-technical environments; and an ability to identify and use appropriate technical literature;		Direct individual method: Evaluate each student’s own spoken technical explanation and responses to questions. Enter a direct individual Communication rating and observation.

Supporting rubric evidence: Individual Presentation (/5) and individual Project Knowledge (/5), with group Presentation Quality (/5) as supporting context.
SO 5 — Teamwork
An ability to function effectively as a member as well as a leader on technical teams.		Direct individual method: Enter an individual Teamwork rating based on observed team responsibilities, collaboration, accountability, and leadership. Record the assigned role/task, leadership, or peer evidence used in the judgment.

Supporting rubric evidence: Individual Contribution (/5) and Accountability & Teamwork (/5), supplemented by documented assigned tasks or peer evidence.
Individual attainment criterion: A direct 0–5 evidence rating is converted as rating ÷ 5 × 100. The program individual threshold is 70% (3.5/5); a 4 or 5 meets it (4/5 = 80%), and a 3 is 60% and does not meet it. The lower 3/5 suggestion on the original group rubric is not the program’s threshold.

Program target: At least 75% of assessed students must meet the individual threshold. Percentages are based on named students with a direct rating for that outcome. Blank evidence means not assessed, not failure.

2. Recording and faculty review

  1. Open Project Rubric and score the project categories and individual contribution, knowledge, presentation, and teamwork.
  2. In “Direct individual outcome evidence,” rate each named student’s performance and document the observation: technical explanation, oral communication, team role/task, or leadership.
  3. Save the team, then use “Faculty review for official outcome use” after checking the record. An unreviewed or earlier proposed record does not enter official SO attainment results.
  4. Use the Reports & graphs tab to view course-grade results separately from direct SO attainment and export detailed evidence for internal faculty use.

3. Preserved project-management content

Project Planning & Work Breakdown Structure, Schedule & Gantt Chart, and Budget & Bill of Materials remain strong evidence of course-specific project-management and economics learning. No new program Student Outcome, SO 2/SO 4 mapping, or numbered official Performance Indicator is claimed for these areas.
